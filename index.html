Meet Mariam

Decor with a soft heart

hello, I'm Mariam

Blush Hues is a Toronto-based event decor studio built on a simple idea: a celebration should feel like the people it's for. Think garden roses in dusty pink, candles at dusk, blush drapery and balloons in every shade of sweet.

From intimate bridal showers at home to full wedding stages, every setup is designed around your story, your colours and your budget — then styled by hand on the day, so you can simply arrive and enjoy it.
